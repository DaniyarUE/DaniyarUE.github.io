CH 01 · DISTRIBUTION

0 → 207K in 28 days

A gaming channel built on real gameplay. I launched it, grew it to 280K and sold it. No team, no budget.

2026
Subscribers, 28 days+207K
Views, 28 days22.1M
Total when sold280K
AwardSilver Creator

I didn't grow it by posting more. I grew it by reading the analytics every day and treating every upload as a test. Most of the work was deciding what to kill.

  1. Hook first. Several versions of the opening seconds per idea, judged on retention, not on how good they looked to me.
  2. Read the drop-off. Every day in YouTube Analytics: where viewers left, which titles and thumbnails got clicked, which formats held.
  3. Scale only what held. Formats that kept people got repeated and varied. Everything else was cut, fast.
  4. Sold it. Grew it to 280K subscribers, then sold the channel.
CH 02 · BUILD

An AI sales agent in 2 days

Muras — my solo startup. I built it, sold it to three companies, then shut it down on purpose.

2026
First agent live2 days
Companies sold to3
Built byMe, solo

Sales teams sit on a CRM full of buyers they forget about. At a luxury food retailer, a manager types one line into Telegram and the agent pulls from the CRM who to call, why, and what it's worth. Bigger orders, from customers nobody would have remembered to call.

The CEO got his own version: revenue, profit and who bought what — any hour, without opening the CRM or waiting for the accountant to pick up.

The first agent was about half the work. The other half was learning each new company's operations. I sold variants to a food retailer, a showroom and an agency.

Why I shut it down: every client needed its own deep custom logic, so nothing carried over from one to the next. It was a services business pretending to be a product. I'd rather know that early.

In my words: the sales and the shutdown

Ask: "Which regulars in Dubai haven't ordered in a while? Give me a list with ready messages." Agent: a file of 40 clients with 3+ orders and 60+ days of silence, a personal message for each, the biggest spenders on top — and a warning about what it couldn't verify.

Ask: "Tell this customer when the salmon arrives." Agent: checks the CRM in two number formats, finds nothing, offers two options instead of guessing, logs the task. Client names and numbers hidden.
